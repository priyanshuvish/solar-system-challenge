How to Play

  1. The Sun is on the left — drag planets onto orbits from closest to farthest.
  2. Drag a planet from the tray onto an empty numbered orbit slot.
  3. Correct placements make the planet smile and lock in place (+10 points).
  4. Wrong placements make the planet look sad — then it returns to the tray.
  5. Keyboard: select a planet with Enter, then press 1–8.
  6. Use Hint for a clue about the next empty orbit.
